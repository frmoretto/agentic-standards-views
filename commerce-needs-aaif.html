A view built on the Agentic Standards Map · 16 November 2026

What agentic commerce needs from each AAIF group

Two views of the same public sources. The commerce view follows a purchase made by an agent and says which group should own each question. The overall view sorts every source in the map by the AAIF group that should follow it. Prepared by Francesco Marinoni Moretto.

Sources: the Agentic Standards Map (version 1.5 and the corrections published on 2026-11-16, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23357361). Each chip links to its public entry and shows the date it was read. Which group should own which question is my reading, offered for correction; it is not a position of AAIF or of any of its working groups. The Agentic Standards Map itself ranks nothing and recommends nothing: this view is a separate reading built on its data.

The groups used here are a working frame by the author: Agentic Commerce, four cross-cutting areas (security and privacy with identity, observability and tracing, governance risk and compliance, taxonomy and landscape) and attestation and verification. They are not the structure of AAIF; see the AAIF working group repositories for that. "Primary, secondary, feedback" is the author's proposal of who leads, who contributes and who reviews. "Internet of agents" is not a group: it collects the protocols and discovery work no single group owns.

Limits: each status is as read on the date shown, not today. The map covers public sources only. Of the AAIF hosted projects, the map covers MCP and A2A; Agent Skills, OpenSandbox, OpenEnv, Goose, agentgateway and Agent Router are not covered. The "what to test" lines are questions for the Accuracy and Reliability group, not evaluation criteria. Disclosure: the author takes part in some of the work cited (UCP, IETF AUDIT and vCon among others); see the map's disclosure page.
