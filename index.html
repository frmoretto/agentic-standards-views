Agentic Standards Map views

Thematic views built on the data of the Agentic Standards Map. The map records who is standardising agentic commerce and ranks nothing. A view is a separate reading of the same sources from one angle, and says so.

  • What agentic commerce needs from each AAIF group
    For each step of a purchase made by an agent: which AAIF working group should own the question, what commerce needs from it, and the public work that addresses it. Data: map version 1.5 and the corrections of 2026-11-16.

By Francesco Marinoni Moretto (ORCID). Independent work; not a deliverable of AAIF or of any body it describes. Content under CC BY 4.0. Corrections and proposals: issues on the repository; corrections to the underlying entries go to the map's repository.
